# Metropolitan State University Portal - Login Credentials

## 📋 Login Information

The portal has been designed with **test credentials** for demonstration purposes. Use the following credentials to access different user roles:

---

## 👤 **STUDENT LOGIN**

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Role** | Student |
| **Username/ID** | `student` |
| **Password** | `pass` |

**Dashboard Access:** Student Portal with tabs for Timetable, Grades, Fee Statement, Profile, and Learning Materials

---

## 👨‍🏫 **LECTURER/FACULTY LOGIN**

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Role** | Lecturer |
| **Username/ID** | `lecturer` |
| **Password** | `pass` |

**Dashboard Access:** Faculty Portal with tabs for My Classes, Student List, Enter Grades, My Timetable, and Announcements

---

## 🔐 How to Login

1. Click the **"Login"** button in the navigation bar
2. Select your role from the dropdown (**Student** or **Lecturer**)
3. Enter the **Username/ID** from the table above
4. Enter the **Password** from the table above
5. Click **"Login"** button

---

## 📱 Portal Features

### **STUDENT DASHBOARD** includes:
- ✅ Class Timetable with times, subjects, lecturers, and rooms
- ✅ Grades overview with coursework, exam marks, and GPA
- ✅ Fee Statement showing payment status and balance
- ✅ Student Profile with personal information
- ✅ Learning Materials with downloadable resources

### **LECTURER/FACULTY DASHBOARD** includes:
- ✅ My Classes - Enrolled students and class information
- ✅ Student List - View students with attendance and grades
- ✅ Enter Grades - Input coursework and exam marks
- ✅ My Timetable - Teaching schedule and sessions
- ✅ Announcements - Post and manage class announcements

---

## 🌙 **DARK MODE**

Click the **moon icon (🌙)** in the top-right corner to toggle dark mode. Your preference is automatically saved!

---

## 📝 **APPLICATION FORM** (Public)

You can also fill out the **Admission Portal** form without logging in:
- Fill in your full name, email, phone, and NRC number
- Select your desired programme (BS Computer Science, BS Business Admin, BS Nursing, etc.)
- Write a personal statement
- Click "Submit Application"
